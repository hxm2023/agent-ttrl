## Adversarial Review Gate (try to kill the WINNER)

**R1 — Essential difference from closest competitor (CVT-RL 2606.05263)?**
CVT-RL: training-time constrained policy-gradient RL; PCCC counterfactual credit (deletion/
semantic/evidence/tool-output interventions) with doubly-robust selection-adjusted
estimation; intervention-validity gating; full verifiable reward; NO deployment loop, NO
commit gate, NO partial-evidence restriction, NO prequential/future-transfer evaluation.
Ours: deployment-period stream; ONLY E_hard/E_soft enter adaptation (R_hidden excluded);
reliability/conflict-gated signed credit drives session LoRA updates; every candidate
passes a statistical commit gate with anchor retention before affecting future tasks;
primary metric is AUPC_prequential on never-updated tasks. Delta = deployment +
evidence tiers + commit gate + prequential. VERDICT: survives, conditional on matched
controls (deployment-adapted CVT-RL-style credit baseline in Block 2).

**R2 — From the second-closest (PACE 2606.08106)?**
PACE: prompt-level self-modification proposals; e-process anytime-valid acceptance on a
small held-out set; no parameters, no RL, no stateful environments, no anchor retention,
no partial-evidence candidates. Ours: LoRA candidates generated by partial-evidence RL;
gate controls contamination AND anchor harm AND catastrophic updates; sentinel reuse
limits + cross-candidate error budgets. Delta = parameter-level RL-loop commit under
partial evidence. VERDICT: survives; M2 adopts PACE's e-process as a gate variant (we
must not run a strictly weaker fixed-n gate without the e-process comparison).

**R3 — Why not the standard alternative (critic/value model, vanilla GRPO, majority vote)?**
At deployment time there are no labels to fit a value model and the distribution shifts;
self-consistency rewards (TTRL) are exactly what T3RL/Amplification showed can reinforce
wrong consensus. Matched-branch evidence credit is the alternative to self-consistency;
all three families (inference, context, RL baselines) are run under identical caps.
VERDICT: survives.

**R4 — Is the gain only from more compute/rollout/SNR?**
Pre-registered controls: equal-extra-rollout (give branch compute to plain rollouts),
random branch, unpaired continuation, terminal-only, hard-evidence-only. Mechanism gate:
credit sign accuracy ≥10pp over random/unpaired on CTS exact oracle + rank corr ≥0.15
(design doc §5.6). If random/equal-extra matches → claim shrinks to exploration/repair
benefit. VERDICT: survives, conditionally (mechanism gate is binding).

**R5 — Why domain-specific (would it work on math)?**
The mechanism depends on (i) E_hard state invariants/schema/receipts that encode
verifiable consequences of tool calls, (ii) replayable environment snapshots for paired
branches, (iii) collateral damage/irreversibility structure — none exist in math CoT. On
math the method degenerates to T3RL-style weighting (which is why math TTRL is a baseline,
not a claim). CTS is built to be agent-specific. VERDICT: survives.

**R6 — Does the headline metric predict the thing it claims?**
AUPC_prequential IS the claim (first-attempt hidden outcome on tasks never touched by
update/selector/gate). Additional pre-registered diagnostic: credit-fidelity (CTS sign
accuracy/rank) must positively correlate with prequential gain across streams; if not,
the mechanism story (C1) shrinks to "exploration/repair benefit" regardless of main-table
gains. VERDICT: survives; diagnostic is binding.

**R7 — How is this not Tree-RL / aTTT / StarOR?**
Tree-RL: training-time, full reward, no gate. aTTT: TTT (no reward/RL), no evidence tiers,
no gate, no prequential; its repetition filter is a drift symptom fix, not a commit gate.
StarOR: per-instance (no stream), optimization modeling, MCTS comparison sets, no partial
evidence tiers, no gate, no future-transfer evaluation. Table in related work, plus the
matched-deployment controls. VERDICT: survives.

**Gate verdict**: **SURVIVES** with the conditions above (M1-M4 + binding mechanism and
fidelity diagnostics + deployment-adapted matched controls for CVT-RL/Tree-RL-style credit
and PACE-style gate). Any condition violated at pilot (M3) ⇒ revert per design doc §4.3.
