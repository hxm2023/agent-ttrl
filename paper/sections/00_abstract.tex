% Abstract
\begin{abstract}
Deployed tool-using agents receive partial, endogenous feedback and
must improve on future tasks without contaminating behavior. We study
deployment-period, session-scoped LoRA reinforcement learning under a
strict prequential protocol with evidence tiers (hard/soft evidence in
adaptation; the hidden evaluator never) and three-channel budget
accounting. We instantiate EGC-TTRL, which pairs counterfactual
branches at selected decision states to produce signed,
reliability-gated action credit, and gates candidate adapters through a
risk-controlled commit gate (empirical-Bernstein e-process) before they
reach future tasks. The protocol machinery runs end-to-end on CTS,
AppWorld and tau2 with Qwen3-4B and Mistral-7B; SafeCommit eliminates
catastrophic updates in stress simulation while remaining
non-degenerate. Our empirical synthesis is honest: at the scales and
update magnitudes tested, deployment-period LoRA-RL yields no stable
inductive-future-transfer gain. We contribute the validated protocol
machinery, the commit-gate result, and a fully reproducible harness
with all run manifests.
\end{abstract}
